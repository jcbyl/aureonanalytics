Core Services

What we test

Environmental Analysis

Water, soil, and air testing for regulatory compliance, site assessment, and remediation monitoring.

  • Soil & solid waste characterization

Chemical & Materials

  • Potency & compositional analysis
  • Residual solvents (GC-MS)

  • Product quality & stability

Data & Reporting

The Laboratory

An independent lab, built for PR

Aureon Analytics is the analytical testing division of Aureon Scientific Holdings, a Puerto Rico life-science infrastructure holding company. We exist because the island's growers, producers, and projects need testing capacity that is close, credible, and independent — data that holds up to regulators and counterparties alike.

Our practice spans the environmental, agricultural, and industrial sectors. As the laboratory scales, our scope and certifications grow with it — every method validated, every result reviewed.
